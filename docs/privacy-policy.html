Privacy Policy - Time Keeper

Last updated: February 13, 2026

Overview

Time Keeper is an Android application that helps you track the age of people who matter to you. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data Collection

Time Keeper collects only the information you voluntarily provide within the app:

  • Names of people you add
  • Dates of birth you enter
  • Optional descriptions (e.g., "my daughter")
  • Display preferences

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. Time Keeper does not have a server, cloud database, or any external storage. Your data never leaves your device.

Network Usage

The app requires the INTERNET permission solely to download an on-device AI model file (approximately 395 MB) from Hugging Face when you enable the AI messages feature. This is a one-time download. The AI model runs entirely on your device and no personal data is sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

Time Keeper does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting services. No data is shared with any third party.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Device identifiers
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Financial information
  • Health data

Data Deletion

You can delete any person's data directly within the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Time Keeper does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app stores only information that users voluntarily enter.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
